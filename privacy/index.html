Privacy and analytics

Score calculations run in your browser. We do not send scores, study records or answers to Google Analytics.

Optional Google Analytics loads only if you choose “Allow analytics.” It may use analytics cookies and receives page views plus fixed labels for a completed score estimate, visible offer and deliberate checkout click. Arbitrary query parameters, scores, answers, names, emails and checkout references are not sent. Declining prevents the tag from loading. Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control and QA visits suppress it. Use “Analytics settings” in the footer to change your choice.

The paid kit uses a separate first-party action counter and Stripe for payment. Those counters are action totals, not unique people. Payment status is verified by the checkout service, not by a return page.
